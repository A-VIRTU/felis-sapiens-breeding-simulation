Frequently Asked Core Scenarios

What specific breed does the Felis Sapiens program utilize?

The program is fundamentally built upon the European Shorthair (Evropská krátkosrstá kočka). Our founding structural subject, "Šipka", whose exceptional cognitive metrics initiated this lineage, belonged to this breed, making it the organic, inevitable baseline. While other, more exotic breeds demonstrably possess remarkable ethological traits, attempting to engineer extreme IQ by cross-breeding over an already highly artificially-selected exterior breed presents profound technical and ethical hurdles that lack logical justification. Cross-breeding is strictly prohibited within our matrices. The European Shorthair offers a robust, phenomenally healthy physiology—unaltered by extreme cosmetic aesthetics—making it the perfect, enduring, agile vessel for cognitive expansion.

Do these highly cognitive felines require a complex, esoteric diet?

Absolutely not. The subjects operate flawlessly on the exact same nutritional best practices required by any responsible, premium breeder. A high-quality, high-protein standard feline diet is all that is mandated. There are no supernatural or secretive dietary prerequisites—merely a zero-tolerance policy for feeding substandard commercial byproduct fillers.

What can I expect from life with this entity?

Do not expect a "pet" that conforms to human rules or a structured human household order. Our felines represent deeply intelligent, highly plastic entities. You do not acquire them to "raise" them, to set arbitrary behavioral boundaries, or to enforce a dominance-based "human order". To successfully integrate, you must adapt your environment, your mental rigidity, and your daily rhythm to the feline—not the reverse. If you share our philosophy that it is the human's fundamental privilege to observe, accommodate, and connect with their unparalleled ethology, you will experience an unimaginably profound, diary-worthy symbiosis built on absolute mutual respect.

What is the veterinary baseline?

Every single feline authorized for transfer boasts an absolutely flawless medical record. None of our provided animals have ever endured any disease, infection, or physical injury. They are continuously vetted, vaccinated, and treated rigorously according to standard, premium veterinary protocols. Upon clearance, each subject is delivered alongside an exhaustive dossier of official veterinary documentation proving their immaculate health condition from birth.

How much more will keeping three, four, or five cats cost me compared to one?

Logistically (hyper-premium nutrition and veterinary oversight), the cost curve is linear. However, the emotional and temporal Return on Investment grows exponentially. By maintaining a trio or larger pack, your daily requisite "neuro-management" time decreases. Once unified, the pact provides a self-sustaining social matrix. You aren't merely sustaining pets; you are funding an intellect-driven enclave.

Can I travel extensively with the subject?

Our current generations and analogous lineages have not been vetted for relentless daily transit, but they exhibit profound adaptability. Assuming you provide a calm environment and constant human vocal or visual communication (avoiding blind confinement), they manage full-day car journeys and week-long foreign relocations without excessive anxiety. While transit is inherently more stressful than static territory, managing it strictly via the "All-Good" protocol yields healthy psychological exhaustion. Absorbing new vistas exponentially fortifies their self-confidence at home. A universal rule of thumb applies: do not take a Felis Sapiens anywhere you would hesitate to take a two-year-old human child.

We routinely transport our subjects between urban headquarters and a rural testing estate equipped with extensive automation technology. The cats do not necessarily cheer for departures—they may vocalize annoyance initially—but panic is nonexistent. They quickly resort to sleeping. Upon arrival, they showcase absolute arrogance in their fast orientation; simply exposing their feeding array and opening the boundaries initiates uninhibited exploration.

Acquisition Warning: The extreme cognitive awareness of our felines means they predict travel preparation routines flawlessly and instantly seek concealment. You must reserve considerable, placid time exclusively for departure gathering. The more human anxiety or rush they sense, the more brilliant their hiding stratagems become. While they recognize their names impeccably, they will not surrender their position to a verbal recall.

Can I rename the assigned feline subject?

From an emotional and highly subjective standpoint, we prefer you do not. We dedicate significant thought to assigning resonant, significant names reflecting prominent historical personalities, which we firmly believe suit our subjects perfectly. However, if we discard human sentiment and evaluate solely from a physiological and psychological well-being perspective, there is absolutely zero empirical argument indicating that a name change harms the feline in any capacity. Ultimately, much like human beings, they adapt to new affectionate nicknames fluidly and without trauma.

Will I see your felines at public exhibitions?

Categorically, yes. Our subjects are legally required to undergo rigorous phenotype evaluations before sanctioned commissions. This guarantees that alongside extreme cognitive plasticity, we flawlessly respect the physical definitions of the breed. However, we do not exhibit extensively, nor do we attend to market kittens from cages to the public. Our rare appearances serve purely legislative and scaling purposes.
